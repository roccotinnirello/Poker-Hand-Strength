This is not a rigorous project, and was completed as part of my studies during the first year of my A-Levels. 
It was designed with very simple logic in mind, and more as a proof of concept than a genuine poker tool.

You input your hole cards in the format: The rank of the card and then the suit of the card, followed by your other card.
For example, if your hole cards were:
King of Spades & 6 of Diamonds
You would put:
Ks6d
as your cards. This format follows with the successive inputs.
